# Escape the Breach · Year 9 Cybersecurity

An escape room for teams of 2–3 players (20–25 minutes) for the British School Alzira · Xàtiva · Gandia Group. Same engine as Escape the Feed (Year 10).

- Four zones, one per lesson: The Data Vault (you and your data), Trickster Town (social engineering), Hacker Highway (script kiddies), Malware Maze (rise of the bots).
- 8 challenges (match, sort, spot the phish) and 4 junctions with trap doors. Each zone gives a key; THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK opens the Exit Firewall, followed by a turn-based final showdown.
- Hints, “Help us through”, points, stars, certificate and a copyable results report. Progress is saved on the device only.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Content lives in `js/data.js`.

Based on the National Centre for Computing Education (Teach Computing), KS3 Year 9 Cybersecurity, lessons 1–4, Open Government Licence v3.0.
